Prologue

“My kid is a senior and I don’t know where to start.”

If that sounds like your house, you’re in the right place. Applications, essays, deadlines, scholarships, financial aid: it adds up fast, and most families are figuring it out for the first time. You don’t have to do it alone, and you don’t need to buy a big package to get real help.

Work with me for one session or for the whole season. We go at the pace your student needs, and every session ends with clear next steps.

Every student has a story worth telling. My job is to help them find it, shape it, and get it in front of the right readers. — Next Chapter: College Coaching

How I help

Where students get stuck, and where I come in

Scholarship & application writing

Brainstorming, outlining, drafting, and polishing for college essays, personal statements, and scholarship applications, in the student’s own voice.

College research

Building a balanced list of schools that fit your student’s goals, learning style, and budget, then comparing programs, campuses, and costs side by side.

Figuring out “all that stuff”

Deadlines, applications, FAFSA, recommendation letters, and checklists. We sort out what matters and what to do next, in order.

The story so far

Turning the chapter from high school to college

Every student’s path is different, but the story usually unfolds like this. We start wherever you are.

  1. I
    Chapter One

    Discover

    We talk through interests, strengths, goals, and worries so the plan starts with the student, not a template.

  2. II
    Chapter Two

    Research

    Explore schools, majors, and costs. Build a shortlist and a calendar of deadlines.

  3. III
    Chapter Three

    Write

    Essays and scholarship applications: brainstorm, draft, revise. Strong writing comes from honest ideas and good editing.

  4. IV
    Chapter Four

    Apply & Fund

    Submit with confidence, chase scholarships, and understand financial aid offers.

  5. V
    Chapter Five

    Turn the Page

    Decide, commit, and prepare for the transition to campus life.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do I have to buy a package?

No. You pay by the hour for the sessions you book. There’s no required package or upfront commitment.

Will you write my student’s essays?

No. Essays should sound like the student. I help with brainstorming, structure, and revision, so the final work is theirs and gets better.

What if my student is a senior and the deadlines are close?

Reach out right away. We can prioritize the nearest deadlines first and build a catch-up plan.

Is in person worth the extra $20?

Many students find that face-to-face sessions make complicated topics easier to grasp. Zoom is still a great option, so choose what suits your family.

Do you only work with seniors?

Seniors are the most common, but younger students who want to plan ahead are welcome too.
