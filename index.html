The story so far

Your college timeline, in chapters

Every student’s path is different, but it usually unfolds like this.

  1. Ch.1

    Discover

    Talk through interests, strengths, and goals so the plan starts with the student.

  2. Ch.2

    Research

    Build a balanced school list and a calendar of deadlines.

  3. Ch.3

    Write

    Common App, honors, and scholarship essays: brainstorm, draft, revise.

  4. Ch.4

    Apply & fund

    Submit with confidence, chase scholarships, and compare aid offers.

  5. Ch.5

    Turn the page

    Decide, commit, and get ready for the move to campus.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do I have to buy a package?

No. You pay by the hour for the sessions you book, with no required package or upfront commitment.

Will you write my student’s essays?

No. Essays should sound like the student. I help with brainstorming, structure, and revision so the final work is theirs, and better.

My student is a senior and deadlines are close. Can you still help?

Reach out right away. We’ll tackle the nearest deadlines first and build a catch-up plan.

Is in person worth the extra $20?

Many students find face-to-face sessions make complicated topics easier to grasp. Zoom is a great option too, so choose what suits your family.

Do you only work with seniors?

Seniors are the most common, but juniors are welcome. The Junior Meeting is a good place to start planning ahead.
